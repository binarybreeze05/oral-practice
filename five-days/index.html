Five days

304 prompts in 5 groups · Your boxes ↗
Day 1Day 2Day 3Day 4Day 5

All 304 TEF prompts (speaking A 78, speaking B 81, writing A 72, writing B 73) in five groups of about 61, one group per day before the exam. Each group gathers the prompts with the same subject matter, so the ideas, vocabulary and arguments you prepare for one carry over to the rest of that day, across speaking and writing.

Inside each day, topics put the closest prompts side by side. Within a topic the prompts run speaking A, speaking B, writing A, writing B, each by estimated frequency (most likely first). Prompts that share one prepared answer stay on the same day and in the same topic. Click a speaking prompt to open its ad (English | French).

How the groups were made

One agent sorted all 304 prompts into five groups of 58 to 64 without any other material. Its groups became written rules, and eight agents re-placed every prompt against them, about 38 each (302 of 304 placed the same). Then one auditor per day re-read that day with only a one-line scope per group, flagged the prompts that fit another day better (6, all moved) and split the day into topics. A separate judge settled four volunteering and job sets that could sit on day 4 or day 5. Sizes were checked after every step; the days end at 59 to 64.

Day 1 · Sport, adventure & travel

64 prompts · speaking A 26 · speaking B 21 · writing A 15 · writing B 2

Almost every prompt is about leaving the daily routine for something new: a first try at a sport with an instructor, a trip by boat, train, bike or plane, or a stay somewhere different. The same arguments carry across the day: safety and trained guides, fresh air and nature, time with family or friends, and a low price for a lasting memory. The stories mostly turn on a trip or vehicle that goes wrong, such as a missed train, a turned-back flight or a rescue at sea.
