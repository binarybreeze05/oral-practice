How they were picked
  • What counts: the NCLC 7 writing rules (news-story moves such as the journalistic conditional, a quote with a confié after it, Il s'est avéré que; visible structures such as a participial clause, a past infinitive, dont, a passive; links that mark cause, consequence or concession; precise words and idioms not printed in the prompt).
  • Every sentence judged on its own: 5 judges, 155 different sentences from 18 answers (EA-070 and EA-071 repeat EA-030 and EA-016). 90 lines scored 4 or 5 out of 5.
  • Those were re-scored side by side, near-duplicates removed (same move, same structure: the best one stays), then ranked; lines that travel to other stories rank higher.
  • Why 21: that is where the list stops adding. After the 16 lines scored 5 come 5 at 4 that each add a frame the others lack (finirait par, s'en ressentir, Il s'en est suivi, Il s'est avéré que, an opening backstory); the next lines would repeat a quote, a subjunctive or a plus-que-parfait already listed. None of your answers uses the journalistic conditional (le coffre aurait appartenu à…), so no line shows it.
  • Each French line is copied from its answer, unchanged; the English is your own English for it.
